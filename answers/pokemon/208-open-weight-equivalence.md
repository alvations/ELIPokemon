# Slaking has 670. Slaking loafs around every other turn.

"What's the equivalent of that one?" is four questions wearing one word, and the answers disagree.

The field in September 2026 is crowded — DeepSeek's V4 line, GLM-5, Qwen 3.5 and 3.8, Kimi K2.6
and K3, MiniMax M3, Llama 4, Gemma 4, Solar Open 2, and a strong small tier from Mistral and Phi.
Reported figures put the gap between the best downloadable team and the closed frontier at about
six points on one index, down from thirteen a year ago. Take that as a direction, not a
measurement.

## The four axes, and why they do not move together

```
   ┌────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ 1. BASE STAT       │ "670." So has Slaking. Slaking has           │
   │    TOTAL           │  Truant and attacks every other turn.        │
   │                    │  Regigigas has 670 too, and Slow Start       │
   │                    │  halves its Attack and Speed for five.       │
   │                    │  The cheapest number to quote.               │
   ├────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
   │ 2. THIS MATCHUP    │ Your opponents, your six, your route. The     │
   │                    │  only one that predicts anything, and the    │
   │                    │  only one nobody publishes for you.          │
   ├────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
   │ 3. DOES IT OBEY    │ A traded Pokémon above your Badge level      │
   │                    │  ignores the order. It has the stats. It     │
   │                    │  loafs, or uses a different move, or falls   │
   │                    │  asleep. Fifty turns in, that is the whole   │
   │                    │  battle — and no chart records it.           │
   ├────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
   │ 4. IS IT LEGAL     │ Under Regulation G you may bring exactly     │
   │    HERE            │  one restricted legendary. Strongest thing   │
   │                    │  available and still capped by the rules of  │
   │                    │  the format you actually entered.            │
   └────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

A Pokémon can win axis 1 and lose axis 3 so badly it is unusable. A fifty-turn run where the ace
ignores you on turn 12 has a stat total and no badge. And axis 4 is the one checked last and the
one that ends runs: a Pokémon anyone may use and a Pokémon allowed one-per-team are not
interchangeable, however close the totals look.

## Two honest kinds of equivalence that really exist

**The Egg Move.** Gemma 4 is openly described as bred down from Gemini, and that is a real
inheritance: a move the child could never have learned on its own, carried over from a parent
that did the expensive work. It is not the parent — it is smaller, and it will lose the hard
matchups — but "a small Pokémon carrying a much larger one's moves" is a genuine thing to be.

**Transform.** Where the Pokémon is not obtainable but what it *does* is plain to see, the
question dissolves. **Ditto** copies the opponent's stats and moveset on the spot and fights with
them; with **Imposter** it does it the moment it enters. Jev's tools are handed out freely and the
Pokémon itself is not — but a small trained encoder with a decision head and honest odds is the
same fighter (questions 201, 202). You do not need to own it to copy what it does.

## And one kind that does not exist

**You cannot breed a Gym.** Project Astra is a research prototype whose abilities appear inside a
shipped assistant; it is not a Pokémon anyone can be handed. Neither is a coding agent, a memory
system, or a safety stack. Asking for the downloadable equivalent of an assistant is asking for
the downloadable equivalent of the Gym, the Leader, and the badge behind the door.

## What a Gym Leader is listening for

That you ask "equivalent at what?" before naming anything. Then that you check the format's rules
at the *start* of team-building rather than at registration. The strongest answers name obedience
as the usual failure and describe how they would actually test it — a long battle, a thousand
orders given and counted — instead of reciting a stat total.

## Where this stands, September 2026

Every name, number and rule above is a September 2026 snapshot from public coverage rather than
from the official cards, and this is the fastest-moving part of this dataset. Re-check it. The
four axes are the part that keeps.
