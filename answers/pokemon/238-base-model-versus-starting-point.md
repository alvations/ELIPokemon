# A **Helix Fossil** is a rock in your bag. What **Cinnabar Island** hands back is not.

There are two things in **Kanto** that people call "the fossil" and they are not the same object.
One is the **Helix Fossil** you dig out of **Mt. Moon** — an item, sitting in the bag next to the
**Escape Rope** and the **Town Map**. It has no HP bar. It has no entry on the **Type Chart**. It
cannot hold a **Leftovers** and it will not fit in a **Poké Ball**. The other is what the
scientist at the **Cinnabar Island** lab gives you back across the counter, which is an
**Omanyte**, and is a Pokémon in every sense there is. "A good fossil to build on" is a sentence
about the first. It is almost always said about the second.

## What the counter at **Cinnabar Island** actually hands you

I could not get the scientist to say a word about his method. What I could read is the thing he
put in my hand, and that settles it without him.

It is an **Omanyte**, **Rock** and **Water**, at level 30 in **Red** and **Blue**. Its move slots
are already filled from its own level-up list — you picked none of them. From the third generation
onward it also arrives with its Nature already fixed and its Ability already decided, and changing
that Ability later costs you an **Ability Capsule**. It will become **Omastar** at level 40
whether or not that is what you wanted, unless you hand it an **Everstone**. And because it is
**Rock** and **Water**, **Giga Drain** hits it for four times damage the moment it is on the
field, which is a fact about a Pokémon and not a fact about a rock.

Compare the **Old Amber** from the back room of the museum at **Pewter City**. In the bag it is
inert. Out of the lab it is an **Aerodactyl**: **Rock** and **Flying**, a **Base Stat Total** of
515, 130 base Speed — fast enough to move first against almost anything in **Kanto** — and there
is no next stage for it to reach. Two items, two revivals, two completely different creatures, and
the word "fossil" covering all four.

## Why the difference decides what you are allowed to judge

```
   WHAT YOU ARE HOLDING              WHAT YOU CAN HONESTLY JUDGE ABOUT IT
   ───────────────────────────────   ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
   still in the rock, at Mt. Moon    nothing. you have not got it yet
        │                            ──  NOT: which of the two it is
        │
        ▼
   in your bag                       which Pokémon it will become · that you took
   (Helix, Dome, or Old Amber)       one and the Super Nerd took the other
        │                            ──  NOT: HP, Speed, matchups, anything at all
        │
        ▼
   on the counter at Cinnabar        ──  the revival already happened here
        │
        ▼
   level 30, in your party           everything the Pokédex prints: typing, Base
   Omanyte · Rock / Water            Stat Total, the moves it arrived with, Nature
        │                            ──  AND: every one of those can still be undone
        ▼
   level 40                          Omastar. different stats, same Original Trainer
```

Read that downwards and the first mistake is obvious. **Taking a Helix Fossil into the Battle
Tower and reporting that it lost is not a measurement.** It is a category error. The fossil has no
Speed to be outrun and no typing to be hit; the result you wrote down is a fact about the fossil
being a fossil. The honest instruments for a thing in the bag are the ones that work on items:
which lab takes it, which Pokémon it becomes, and whether you can still get the other one. That
last one is a real answer — you cannot. The **Super Nerd** in **Mt. Moon** takes whichever of
**Helix Fossil** and **Dome Fossil** you leave behind, and **Kabuto** is off the table for that
save file from the moment you choose.

Read it upwards and you get the expensive mistake, which is the one the **Cinnabar Island**
counter actually invites. **What you were handed is not blank.** The level-30 **Omanyte** already
came with moves in its slots, and once all four are full the way you put **Hydro Pump** in is by
pushing something else out. Nobody warns you at the counter. There is no undo. A Pokémon that
arrives shaped can be unshaped, and "I was given the weights" is not the same as "I was given a
clean slate".

## The sixty-second check, on anything somebody calls a fossil

1. **Does it have an HP bar?** If yes, the revival already happened. Stop calling it a fossil.
2. **Can you give it a held item?** A **Helix Fossil** cannot take a **Hard Stone**. **Omanyte**
   can. That one question separates the two objects every time.
3. **Does it appear in the party screen or in the bag?** They are different screens for a reason.
4. **Does it already know moves?** Nothing in the ground knows **Water Gun**. A filled move list
   means somebody filled it, and it was not you.
5. **Is there a second version of it on offer** — one raw and one revived? **Blaine** sells
   neither, and when a lab offers only one, look at which one rather than at what it is called.

The **Helix Fossil** answers 1–4 no. What **Cinnabar Island** hands back answers 1–4 yes. A lab
that offers both says so; question 219 is about not confusing the shelf a thing sits on with the
thing, and this is the same discipline one shelf further in.

## What a Gym Leader is listening for

That you can tell an item from a Pokémon when both are being called the same word, and that you
name the instrument that goes with each — the **Pokédex** for one, the bag for the other. Good
answers notice that the **Super Nerd** makes the choice permanent. The best notice the asymmetry:
a fossil's battle record understates what it will be worth, and a revived **Omanyte**'s move slots
overstate what will still be there after you have finished with it.

## Where this stands, September 2026

The **Mt. Moon** choice, the **Super Nerd**, the level-30 revival in **Red** and **Blue**, the
**Old Amber** at **Pewter City**, **Aerodactyl**'s 515 and its 130 base Speed, and **Omanyte**
becoming **Omastar** at 40 are all from the games and do not rot. Which lab is currently taking
which fossil, and whether anyone is offering the raw one at all, is something each release states
about itself and changes every few years. **The instruction that produced this answer called the
thing on the counter a fossil. It is not one** — check 1 and check 2 above are how that was
settled rather than assumed. Open the bag, then open the party screen, and see which one it is in.
