# The whole arrangement is printed on a board at the gate

Questions 222 and 227 are about deals that check something *later* — a gate that looks at how far
along you are, in the middle of a fight, on a turn you needed. The **Safari Zone** at **Fuchsia
City** is the other kind, and the difference is not that it asks less of you. It is that
**everything it asks is written on the board before you go in**, and nothing is added afterwards.
500 for the game. Thirty **Safari Ball**s. Five hundred steps. Nobody looks at your **Boulder
Badge**, nobody asks how many **Gym**s you have cleared, and nobody comes to find you in
**Blackthorn City** eight hours later.

## The four things the board actually says

* **What you keep.** Whatever you catch is yours, outright, for good. A **Chansey** caught on the
  far side of the fence is a **Chansey** in your party at the **Pokémon League**, at the **Battle
  Tower**, and in the **Hall of Fame**. There is no clause that reaches back in. There is no level
  at which it stops obeying and no total at which somebody wants a word.
* **What you are handed that you could not get anywhere else.** Two moves live inside that fence
  and nowhere else in **Kanto**: **Surf**, sitting in the Secret House, and the **Gold Teeth**,
  which the **Warden** trades for **Strength**. Both are **HM**s, both open routes that are
  otherwise shut, and both come with the 500. That is not a courtesy. It is the most valuable part
  of the deal and it is in the same paragraph as the entry fee.
* **What travels with the Pokémon.** The summary screen records where it was met, and that line
  does not come off. Pass a **Tauros** along to somebody and the **Safari Zone** goes with it.
* **What is not yours.** A **Safari Ball** works inside the fence and nowhere else. Carrying one
  out to **Route 1** gets you nothing, and a patch of **Tall Grass** you happen to like is not a
  **Safari Zone** however many **Nidoran** are standing in it. The place keeps its own name.

Nothing is promised about how it goes. A Safari Pokémon can turn and leave on any turn, frequently
does, and there is no refund.

The bottom row is the point. Most Trainers were never going to hit the level where the other kind
of gate bites either. What the board removes is the *checking* — the counting of badges, the
watching of levels, the question of whose gift this was. It turns a running worry into a fact.

## What the board conspicuously does not change

1. **You got a Pokémon, not the Safari Zone.** Nobody hands you the fence, the four areas or the
   encounter table. You cannot make another **Kangaskhan** appear, and nobody will tell you why
   that one was standing there.
2. **Nothing is guaranteed.** You may not send out your own Pokémon at all. Your entire toolkit is
   a **Safari Ball**, **Bait**, a **Rock**, and running away. **Bait** makes it stay and makes it
   harder to catch; a **Rock** makes it easier to catch and makes it want to leave. Thirty balls
   and a **Dratini** on the **Super Rod** is a hope, not a plan.
3. **The five hundred steps are the real gate.** Everybody may walk in. Only somebody willing to
   spend the walk reaches the far area where the **Chansey** are, and a Trainer who runs out of
   steps two squares short is shown the gate with **Safari Ball**s still in the bag.
   **The board removes the gate at the door and leaves the walking exactly where it was**, and
   people who say the **Safari Zone** is open to everyone usually mean only the door.
4. **The name stays with the place**, and what a **Battle Tower** or a **Pokémon League** will
   allow you to bring is decided somewhere else entirely, by people who never read this board.

## What to actually do

* **Read the board you are standing at**, not the one you remember from **Fuchsia City** last
  year.
* **If you pass the Pokémon on, the met location goes too.** That is not optional and it is the
  most commonly forgotten part of the arrangement.
* **Write down the fee and the day**, on one line. Next visit you only have to check the board is
  the same board.
* **Do not call your own patch of Tall Grass a Safari Zone.**

## What a Gym Leader is listening for

That you name the two **HM**s as the real difference rather than repeating that the fee is small.
Good answers separate what the board permits from what the five hundred steps permit. The best
notice that the value of a printed arrangement is procedural — it deletes the checking — and that
"no refunds if it runs" is where the risk of a place full of **Chansey** and **Tauros** actually
sits.

## Where this stands, September 2026

The 500, the thirty **Safari Ball**s, the five hundred steps, **Bait** and the **Rock**, **Surf**
in the Secret House and the **Gold Teeth** trading for **Strength** are from the games and do not
rot. Whether the gate you are at prints that board — or a different one — is something each place
states about itself, and different regions have run the **Safari Zone** on completely different
rules. Read the board in front of you before you pay.
