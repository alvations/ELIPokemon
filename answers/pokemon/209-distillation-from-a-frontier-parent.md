# The Egg Move transfers. The level does not.

Breed a **Dragonite** and the **Egg** does not hatch into a Dragonite. It hatches into a
**Dratini** at level 1 — and that Dratini can know **Extreme Speed**, a move it could never have
learned on its own, because a parent knew it.

That is distillation, exactly. Gemma 4 is handed out freely and openly described as bred down
from Gemini: a Pokémon anyone can raise, carrying moves from a parent nobody outside Google could
have raised.

## What breeding actually moves

The baby does not copy the parent's *result*. It inherits from the parent's *structure*. A flat
"this one won" tells you almost nothing; a moveset tells you what the parent would have reached
for, and what it would have reached for second. That relative shape is the extra signal, and it
is worth far more than the win.

Three things come across well:

* **Moves and manner.** Egg Moves, the **Nature** if the parent holds an **Everstone**, the
  **Hidden Ability** when a parent has it. This is why a bred Pokémon *feels* like its line long
  before it can fight like one.
* **The parent's breeding work, indirectly.** A **Destiny Knot** passes five **IVs** — you inherit
  the benefit of hundreds of eggs you never hatched.
* **The easy matchups.** Everything the line handles routinely comes across almost whole.

## What does not

```
   ┌── the parent (fully raised) ─────────────────────────────────┐
   │                                                              │
   │   routine battles          hard matchups        rare tricks  │
   │   ████████████████████     ██████████           ███████      │
   └──────────┬───────────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────┘
              │ inherited             │ partial         │ mostly lost
              ▼                       ▼                 ▼
   ┌── the hatchling (level 1) ───────────────────────────────────┐
   │   ████████████████████     ███·······           ·······      │
   └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        ▲                              ▲                  ▲
        │                              │                  └ everything
        │                              │                    that needed
        │                              │                    stat totals
        │                              └ long battles it cannot survive
        └ moves, Nature, manner: nearly free

   And the gap cuts both ways: breed from something ENORMOUSLY
   stronger and the baby does worse than breeding from a middling
   parent. The biggest parent is not automatically the right one.
```

* **The hard matchups.** A level-1 **Dratini** knowing Extreme Speed still has level-1 stats. The
  move is there. The Pokémon that could use it is not.
* **Honest odds.** A hatchling comes out *bolder* than its parent, not more careful — it learned
  what to reach for, not when to doubt. Re-check the percentages after breeding (question 202).
* **Long battles.** A fifty-turn **Battle Tower** run compounds every small gap. Winning one turn
  like the parent and winning a run like the parent are different results.
* **The parent's own ceiling.** No hatchling exceeds what it was shown. Take it somewhere the
  parent never went and the gap reopens immediately.

## The part that is rules, not biology

Gemma-from-Gemini is clean because the same Trainer owns both parents. **You can only breed
within an Egg Group** — and breeding from somebody else's Pokémon, without their say-so, is not a
biology problem, it is a rules problem. Several released teams have been accused of exactly that.
If you plan to breed, check what the parent's owner permits *before* you start hatching, not
after.

## What a Gym Leader is listening for

That you can say what a moveset carries that a win-loss record does not. Then the honest list of
what does not survive — especially that the hatchling comes out overconfident, which almost
nobody mentions. The strongest answers raise the too-strong-parent problem and the ownership
question without being asked.

## Where this stands, September 2026

Gemma 4's particulars are September 2026 and the card is the authority. Breeding is older than
any of these, and the things that fail to transfer have failed to transfer for generations.
