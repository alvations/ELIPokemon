# Writing Unown down in ordinary letters

The Ruins of Alph are covered in Unown. Twenty-six shapes for the twenty-six letters, plus the
one shaped like **!** and the one shaped like **?**. Each shape stands for a letter you already
know, so the walls can be copied out into ordinary writing and read.

That copying-out is transliteration. Nothing is being translated. The words were always the
same words. Only the **marks** changed — and note that all twenty-eight shapes are one species
knowing one move, Hidden Power. The shape was never the identity.

## Why anyone bothers 🔤

Your Pokédex has an entry for every ordinary letter. It has nothing for Unown shapes at all, so
faced with a wall of them it does what any device does with marks it cannot read: it renders
each shape as a clump of meaningless strokes and burns four slots on every single character.

Copy the wall into ordinary letters first and the same sentence arrives in a form the Pokédex
has spent its whole life reading.

```
   THE WALL, LEFT AS IT IS        THE WALL, COPIED OUT
   ───────────────────────        ────────────────────
   ᴜɴᴏᴡɴ shapes                   "ESCAPE"
   → unreadable marks             → six ordinary letters
   → 4 slots per shape            → one clean word
   → Pokédex has no idea          → Pokédex knows this already
```

📌 The rule: **copy it out when the alternative is unreadable marks. Leave it alone when the
Pokédex reads the original perfectly well.** Rewriting a script your Pokédex already knows
throws away information for nothing.

## The trip back is not the trip there ↩️

Going *to* ordinary letters is mechanical. Going *back* is guesswork, and this is the part
people underestimate.

Take Meowth, whose name in another region's writing copies out as *Nyarth*. Go back the other
way from **Meowth** and no rule on earth returns you to the original marks. Bulbasaur is worse:
**Fushigidane** in one region, **Bisasam** in another, **Bulbizarre** in a third, one Pokémon.
Pikachu, for once, is Pikachu everywhere — which is why Pikachu is the only name that ever
round-trips cleanly.

One original, many copies. Many copies, no way home. The return journey is not a lookup, it is
a **guess with context**, and it needs to be treated as one.

## The Braille problem 🔡

The Sealed Chamber's walls are Braille. You can copy them into ordinary letters and read the
instruction — *dig here* — and that works: you Dig, and the chamber opens.

Regirock, Regice and Registeel are all sitting behind instructions written that way, and every
one of them opens for a Trainer who reads the copy rather than the wall.

What you cannot do is copy them out and still have Braille. **The bumps are gone.** Anyone who
needed the bumps has been handed something they cannot use, and your tidy transcription is now
the only surviving copy.

That is the real cost of copying everything out:

* 🌀 **Different words collapse into the same spelling.** Marks that the original kept apart —
  the marks that told you which word this was — get flattened into the same letters, and now
  nobody can tell them apart.
* ✍️ **Everybody spells it differently.** Trainers copying cries into letters produce *Nyarth*,
  *Nyaasu*, *Nyasu* in the same notebook page. No committee ever agreed on one.
* 🧭 **You lose which region it came from.** Once every wall in Johto and Hoenn is in the same
  plain letters, telling the Ruins of Alph from the Sealed Chamber is suddenly hard — and it was
  trivial while the marks were still there. The Alph chambers are the ones with Kabuto, Omanyte,
  Aerodactyl and Ho-Oh laid out in tiles on the floor; strip the walls to plain letters and that
  is the only clue you have left.
* 👑 **You have quietly declared one alphabet the normal one.** Everything gets copied into
  ordinary letters; ordinary letters never get copied into anything. That is a decision about
  whose writing is the default, and it is worth saying out loud rather than letting it happen.

## Where it is simply correct ✅

* 🧾 **Matching names across regions.** Keep the original as the true record, and a copied-out
  version alongside it purely as a matching key, so *Nyarth* and *Meowth* can find each other.
* 🔎 **Searching.** A Trainer typing plain letters into the Pokédex should still find the Unown
  wall and the Braille chamber.
* ⌨️ **Serving the Trainers who already write this way.** Most of Johto types its notes in
  ordinary letters no matter which alphabet the words belong to. Do not "correct" them back
  into the proper script and then wonder why nothing matches — read them as they are.
