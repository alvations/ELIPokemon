# The type chart is the same in every language

Your Alakazam trained in Kanto. Sabrina drilled it, Giovanni tested it, it has never once been
outside Saffron City.

Fly it to Unova and put it in front of Elesa. It has never seen Zebstrika. It cannot read a
single Unovan sign. And it still wins.

## Why? Because it never learned *Kanto*. It learned the type chart 🗺️

Electric does not beat Ground in Kanto and lose to it in Unova. Super effective is 2×
everywhere. STAB is 1.5× everywhere. Speed decides who moves first from Pallet Town to Paldea.

```
    KANTO                                       UNOVA
    ┌─────────────────────────┐                 ┌─────────────────────────┐
    │ Sabrina's Gym           │                 │ Elesa's Gym             │
    │ names: Kadabra, Mr.Mime │                 │ names: Zebstrika, Emolga│
    └───────────┬─────────────┘                 └───────────┬─────────────┘
                │  different names, different signs         │
                └──────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                               ▼
                  ┌─────────────────────────────┐
                  │  THE TYPE CHART             │  identical in both
                  │  Ground > Electric, 2×      │  regions, unchanged
                  │  STAB 1.5×, Speed goes first│
                  └─────────────────────────────┘
                     Alakazam learned THIS in Kanto.
```

The names are local. The **rules are shared**. Everything Alakazam actually learned was in the
shared part, so it walks straight into a Gym it has never seen and applies it.

## The obvious explanation is wrong ❌

You might think it transfers because some words carry over — "Thunderbolt" is written the same
on a Kanto TM and a Unova TM, so Alakazam has a foothold.

Test it. Send that same Alakazam somewhere the *writing itself* is different — the Braille walls
of the Sealed Chamber, or the Unown script in the Ruins of Alph. Not one shared character. It
**still** transfers. So it was never the shared spelling. It was the chart underneath.

## Which parts travel, and which do not 🧳

```
   🏆 What to do about a Ground-type    ← travels perfectly
   🧠 Who is likely to switch, and when ← travels, a bit shaky
   👂 Reading the local signage         ← does NOT travel
   ✍️ Spelling a nickname correctly     ← does NOT travel
```

Big-picture judgement rides along. Anything that depends on the **letters in front of you** —
reading a route sign, writing a Pokédex entry, catching a name — stays behind in Kanto.

## What makes the trip easier or harder ✈️

* 🤝 **How similar the region is.** Kanto to Johto is a short hop — same landmasses, mostly the
  same species, Bugsy's Gym runs on rules your Brock badge already prepared you for. Kanto to
  Paldea is a genuine culture shock: Terastallizing is a mechanic nobody in Kanto has ever seen.
* 📚 **How much of the region was in the Pokédex you grew up with.** An Alakazam whose Pokédex
  covered Unova in detail arrives half-prepared. One whose Pokédex mentions Unova in a footnote
  arrives blind.

## Three ways to do the trip 🎒

**1. 🆓 Just fly there.** Train in Saffron, battle in Nimbasa, hope the chart carries you. Free.
Works surprisingly well. Always your baseline.

**2. 📝 Translate the drills first.** Take every Kanto training tape, have it rewritten with
Unovan names and Unovan species, and drill Alakazam on *that* before it flies. The rewrite is
imperfect — some tapes come back with Zebstrika mislabelled — but drilling on slightly-wrong
Unova beats drilling on perfect Kanto. **This usually wins, and people skip it.**

**3. 🎧 Bring an interpreter into the Gym.** Leave Alakazam exactly as it is, and translate every
Unovan instruction into Kanto terms live, mid-battle. It works. It also means every single turn
now waits on the interpreter, and every mistake the interpreter makes lands in the battle.

## Three honest warnings ⚠️

**🎓 A handful of local lessons beats any of this.** Fifty real battles in Nimbasa City and
Alakazam is better than every clever transfer trick. Never treat zero local training as a goal
when local training is available.

**📉 Adding more regions to the Pokédex can make each one worse.** One Pokédex, fixed number of
pages. Covering Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar and Paldea means fewer
pages each. That is its own problem, and it is a real one.

**🧪 The exam is easier than the region.** Most reports of "Alakazam did fine in Unova" come from
Unovan Gyms whose script was **written in Kanto and translated over**. Those Gyms are gentle in
a way real Nimbasa is not. The true gap is wider than the report says.
